# Trademark Policy

"Droptime", "Droptime Logger", and the Droptime logo are trademarks of
Droptime / Ryan Luttrell.

The AGPL license covers the code. It does not cover the name or the logo.
That distinction is what lets us keep the software genuinely open while
making sure that when a roaster downloads something called "Droptime
Logger", it is actually this project — built, tested, and stood behind by
its maintainers.

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully.** "Compatible with Droptime Logger",
  "imports Droptime Logger data", "a fork of Droptime Logger", "works with
  Droptime Logger" — all fine. This is nominative fair use and we like it.
- **Write about it.** Blog posts, reviews, tutorials, videos, screenshots,
  conference talks. Praise and criticism equally welcome.
- **Build it from source** for yourself, your roastery, or your team, name
  and logo intact, as long as you haven't modified it.
- **Link to us** from anywhere.

## What we ask

- **Forks that ship binaries must rename first.** If you distribute a
  modified version — installers, app bundles, packages, app-store listings —
  pick your own name and your own icon before you ship. Remove "Droptime"
  from the app name, the binaries, and the listing. A "based on Droptime
  Logger" credit in your README is welcome and appreciated.
- **Don't imply endorsement.** Nothing you make should suggest it is
  official, affiliated, or approved by Droptime unless it is.
- **Don't put the marks in your own identity.** Product names, company
  names, domain names, npm packages, and social handles that lead with
  "Droptime" are out.
