## How to Sign

This Agreement is signed electronically. When you open a pull request, the
CLA Assistant bot will ask you to sign if you have not already. To sign, post
the following comment on your pull request, exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature (GitHub username and timestamp) is recorded in this
repository's `cla-signatures` branch. One signature covers all of your past
and future Contributions to Droptime projects; you will not be asked again
unless the Agreement changes.

Questions before signing? Open a discussion or email ryan@outsidethebox.dev —
happy to talk it through.
